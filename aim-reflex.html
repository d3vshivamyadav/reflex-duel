CURSOR CALIBRATION & SENSITIVITY

DPI, Refresh Rate & Input Delay Factors in Browser Aim Training

Two players with identical biological nerves often record a 40 ms gap on browser aim drills simply due to OS pointer settings and display refresh rates. Running Chrome or Safari on a standard 60Hz office screen forces each newly spawned target to wait up to 16.7 milliseconds before lighting up the next scanout frame, whereas a 144Hz or 240Hz gaming panel renders each emerging orb within 4.2 to 6.9 milliseconds—delivering sharper motion tracking as your mouse glides between positions.

Likewise, setting your mouse between 800 DPI and 1600 DPI with Windows Enhance Pointer Precision disabled keeps cursor motion strictly linear on desktop, eliminating variable speed curves during rapid vertical and diagonal snaps. On touchscreen phones and tablets, hovering your dominant thumb slightly outside the orb spawn zone prevents your own finger silhouette from blocking peripheral vision when the next emerald target flashes live inside the zero-scroll arena pad.
